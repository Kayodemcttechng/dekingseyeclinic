Conditions We Treat

Eye Conditions We Diagnose & Treat

From everyday irritation to sight-threatening disease, our specialists treat the full range of ophthalmic conditions.

Cataracts

Clouding of the eye's natural lens, causing blurred or dim vision.

Glaucoma

Raised eye pressure that gradually damages the optic nerve.

Refractive Errors

Short-sightedness, long-sightedness and astigmatism affecting focus.

Dry Eye Syndrome

Insufficient tear quality leading to irritation, grit and discomfort.

Conjunctivitis

Inflammation of the eye's surface, often called pink eye.

Diabetic Retinopathy

Diabetes-related damage to the blood vessels of the retina.

Eye Infections

Bacterial, viral or fungal infections affecting the eye and lids.

Vision Disorders

Blurred, double or distorted vision arising from a range of causes.

The story, the science, and the specialists behind your sight

De Kings Eyes Clinic began as a single consulting room in Lekki Phase 1 and has grown into a full ophthalmic hospital — without losing the personal attention that started it. Here's who we are, why we exist, and the people who show up for your eyes every day.

Our Story

A decade of protecting vision, one patient at a time

How we began

Founded in 2015 by a small team of ophthalmologists who kept seeing the same problem: quality eye care in Lagos was either rushed or out of reach. De Kings Eyes Clinic opened with one consulting room, one refraction unit, and a rule that's stuck ever since — no patient leaves without understanding their own diagnosis.

How we practice

Every diagnosis runs through calibrated, hospital-grade imaging and screening equipment, serviced quarterly to international standards. But the equipment reads the eye — the specialist still reads the patient, which is why every treatment plan is built around your history, not a template.

Where we're headed

We're growing our pediatric and diabetic retina wings and training the next set of Nigerian ophthalmic specialists in-house, so the standard of care we've built doesn't depend on any one doctor being in the room.

A Hospital, Not Just A Clinic

Purpose-built treatment rooms, a dedicated biomedical engineering team, and equipment held to the same standard as a full hospital eye department.

What Drives Us

Mission, Vision & Values

The commitments that shape every appointment, from the front desk to the operating room.

Our Mission

To protect and restore vision through accurate diagnostics, evidence-based treatment, and compassionate care — making world-class eye health accessible to every family we serve.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted eye care and biomedical technology centre in the region — a place where advanced equipment and human warmth meet, in equal measure.

Compassion First

An eye appointment can be frightening. We slow down, explain what we're seeing, and never leave a patient guessing about their own diagnosis.

Precision & Safety

Calibrated equipment, documented protocols, and specialists who double-check before a single procedure begins.

Integrity

Honest pricing, honest timelines, and a plain-language explanation of every option — including the ones we don't get paid more for recommending.

Accessibility

Transparent pricing, flexible plans, and community screening days, so that quality eye care never depends on how much you earn.

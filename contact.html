Before You Reach Out

Quick Answers

A few things worth checking before you call or write in.

How quickly will I get a reply?

Messages sent through this page or by email are typically answered within one business day. For anything urgent, call or WhatsApp us directly — that's the fastest way to reach the front desk.

Can I walk in without an appointment?

Yes, for eye emergencies we see walk-ins immediately. For routine services, booking ahead means shorter wait times, but we'll always try to fit you in the same day if you walk in.

Where exactly are you located?

We're at 15 Admiralty Way, Lekki Phase 1, Lagos — see the map above for directions. On-site parking is available for patients.

Do you accept HMO / health insurance?

We work with several major HMO plans in Nigeria. Message us your HMO name here, or bring your ID to your appointment, and our front desk will confirm your coverage.

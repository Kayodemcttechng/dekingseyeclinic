Conditions We Treat

Eye Conditions We Diagnose & Treat

From everyday irritation to sight-threatening disease, our specialists treat the full range of ophthalmic conditions.

Cataracts

Clouding of the eye's natural lens, causing blurred or dim vision.

Glaucoma

Raised eye pressure that gradually damages the optic nerve.

Refractive Errors

Short-sightedness, long-sightedness and astigmatism affecting focus.

Dry Eye Syndrome

Insufficient tear quality leading to irritation, grit and discomfort.

Conjunctivitis

Inflammation of the eye's surface, often called pink eye.

Diabetic Retinopathy

Diabetes-related damage to the blood vessels of the retina.

Eye Infections

Bacterial, viral or fungal infections affecting the eye and lids.

Vision Disorders

Blurred, double or distorted vision arising from a range of causes.

Common Questions

Services FAQ

The questions we're asked most before a first visit.

Do I need a referral to book an appointment?

No. You can book any service directly — a comprehensive eye examination, cataract or glaucoma screening, or an emergency slot — without a referral from another doctor.

How long does a comprehensive eye examination take?

Most comprehensive examinations take 45–60 minutes, including diagnostic imaging. Pupil dilation, when needed, adds extra time and leaves your near vision blurred for a few hours — plan for someone to drive you home.

What age do you see for pediatric eye care?

Our pediatric team sees children from infancy through the teenage years, including school vision screenings and lazy-eye (amblyopia) detection.

Do you accept health insurance (HMOs)?

We work with several major HMO plans in Nigeria. Bring your HMO ID to your appointment and our front desk will confirm your coverage before you're seen.

What counts as an eye emergency?

Sudden vision loss, a chemical splash, a foreign object in the eye, sudden severe eye pain, or an eye injury all qualify. Call ahead if you can, but you don't need an appointment — walk in and we'll see you immediately.

Not sure which service you need?

Start with a comprehensive eye examination — our specialists will guide you from there.
